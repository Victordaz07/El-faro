# Faro en la Tormenta: arte del Arsenal (ChatGPT)

Todo lo que falta para que el Arsenal se vea con arte propio en lugar de emojis:

- **Parte 1:** 25 íconos de equipo (reliquias, herramientas, provisiones y planos).
- **Parte 2:** 2 botones de herramientas (Barril y Red).
- **Parte 3:** el modelo 3D del buque de guerra.

## Cómo hacerlo

- **Íconos y botones (partes 1 y 2):** abre un chat nuevo en ChatGPT por cada **grupo**, pega el **prompt maestro de íconos** y después los prompts del grupo, uno por uno. Si ChatGPT pierde el estilo, abre un chat nuevo y vuelve a pegar el maestro.
- **Si sale con fondo:** pide *"same image, fully transparent background, PNG"*.
- **Nombres:** descarga cada PNG con el nombre exacto que indica cada prompt (por ejemplo `gear-brujula.png`).
- **Entrega:** mándame todo en un zip. Yo recorto el halo, los convierto a WebP y los conecto.
- **Buque (parte 3):** es un modelo `.glb`, como los barcos anteriores, y usa su propio prompt maestro.

## Prompt maestro de íconos (Arsenal)

```
You are creating 3D game item icons for a mobile tower-defense game called "Faro en la Tormenta" (a lighthouse keeper defending a stormy night sea against sea monsters and pirates).

Rules for every image:
- ONE single 3D object, centered, filling about 80% of the canvas, with empty margin on every side.
- Square 1024x1024 PNG with a FULLY TRANSPARENT background. No circle, no badge, no frame, no card, no ground, no floor shadow, no text, no letters, no numbers.
- Style: stylized, chunky, premium mobile-game 3D (soft clay/toy look with crisp bevels and hand-painted texture detail), slightly exaggerated proportions, like high-end casual game loot icons.
- Lighting: soft key light from the top-left, a cool rim light around the silhouette, gentle inner glow on magical parts. High contrast so it reads at 48 pixels wide on a dark navy background.
- Camera: slight 3/4 view from a bit above, object tilted a little toward the viewer.
- Game palette: deep navy night sea, turquoise accent (#3fd7c4), warm lamp light (#ffe9a8), weathered wood, wet stone, brass-dark metal. Do NOT use shiny gold.
- Rarity is shown by the game with a colored outline, so do NOT add glows, auras or frames that indicate rarity. Legendary items may have their own inner magical glow.
- Keep all effects tight around the object; nothing touches the image edges.

Reply first with a short checklist of these rules, then wait for each asset description.
```

## Parte 3. Buque de guerra en 3D (`barco-guerra.glb`)

Hoy el buque usa el barco de la Travesía. Para uno propio, usa el **prompt maestro de `docs/barcos-y-objetos-3d.md`** y después este:

```
Asset: barco-guerra.glb - the lighthouse keepers' warship, an allied two-deck galleon, sturdier and more armed than a merchant ship.
Size: longest dimension (bow to stern, along +Z) exactly 1.0 unit. Origin at the waterline center. FRONT (+Z) is the bow. Model only the part above the waterline, flat hull bottom at y = 0.
Design: dark polished wood hull with a turquoise stripe (#3fd7c4) along both sides, four cannons on each side poking out of square gun ports, two masts with cream sails bearing a simple turquoise lighthouse emblem, a small turquoise pennant at the top of the main mast, a warm glowing lantern (#ffe9a8) at the stern, a small figurehead of a lighthouse at the bow.
Parts (separate nodes, English names): "Hull", "Sails" (both sails, pivot at the masts so they can sway), "Cannons" (all cannons, pivot at their bases so they can recoil), "Flag" (pennant, pivot at the pole), "Lantern" (stern lantern, emissive).
Materials (max 6): dark wood, light wood deck, turquoise paint, cream sail cloth, dark iron (metallic 0.25), warm emissive lantern glass.
Triangle budget: 3,000 to 7,000.
```
